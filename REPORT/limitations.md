# Technical Limitations, Failure Modes & Edge Cases

A transparent engineering discussion of the failure modes, edge cases, and architectural trade-offs across the four assignment tasks.

---

## 1. Task 1: Pretrained Foundation Segmentation Limitations

1. **Pseudo-Ground-Truth Dependency:**
   - The reference masks generated by Grounding DINO + SAM 2.1 Tiny are empirical foundation model estimates, not manually verified human ground truth.
   - Any subtle boundary inaccuracies produced by SAM (such as under-segmentation along blurry contact points with white plates) propagate into Task 2 and Task 3 evaluation metrics.
2. **Computational Demands:**
   - Vision-language detection and foundation mask decoding require dedicated GPU acceleration (`~1.5 GB` VRAM for Grounding DINO Tiny + SAM 2.1 Tiny). They are unsuitable for real-time edge microcontrollers without model quantization or tensor RT optimization.

---

## 2. Task 2 & Task 3: Classical Image Processing Failure Modes

1. **Pale Background Confusion:**
   - Classical color thresholding relies on CIE LAB luminance and HSV saturation. White ceramic plates, light quartz countertops, and marble surfaces with high luminance and low saturation produce false-positive segmentations, capping precision at $~38\%$.
2. **Touching & Clustered Eggs:**
   - Classical connected-component analysis cannot easily split contiguous eggs sharing identical chromatic properties without learned semantic boundary splitters.
3. **Specular Reflection & Glare:**
   - High-intensity specular reflections create local holes in thresholded masks, which direct algebraic ellipse fitting (E08) or contour closing (E03) struggle to resolve cleanly.

---

## 3. Task 4: Video Tracking Limitations

1. **Identity Fragmentation Under Prolonged Occlusion:**
   - While the tracker exhibits zero false-positive hand hijackings, human hands stopping or covering an egg for $>12$ consecutive frames exceeds the Kalman filter's re-identification memory. When the egg re-emerges, a new track ID is spawned, resulting in 19 total unique track IDs across the 1,441-frame sequence.
2. **Visual Similarity Between Targets:**
   - Raw and hard-boiled eggs in the experiment share identical exterior shell appearances. In the absence of an appearance embedding re-ID feature extractor (e.g., deep cosine metric learning), association relies purely on spatial IoU overlap, which cannot differentiate the two eggs if their spatial trajectories cross in near proximity.
